## Uploaded food safety standard
High-level structure used for coverage:
- management commitment,
- HACCP / food safety plan,
- quality and food safety management system,
- site standards,
- product control,
- process control,
- personnel,
- special production zones where applicable.

## IFS Food Version 8
Public checklist/guidance confirms a systematic HACCP-based food safety management system aligned with Codex principles, GMP/GHP and applicable legal requirements.

## Codex
General Principles of Food Hygiene CXC 1-1969 (2022 revision) is used as the public reference for GHP and HACCP principles.

## EU legal baseline used for product design
- Regulation (EC) 178/2002 - general food law, safety, traceability and responsibilities
- Regulation (EC) 852/2004 - food hygiene and HACCP-based procedures
- Regulation (EU) 1169/2011 - food information and allergen information
- Regulation (EC) 2073/2005 - microbiological criteria
