# PR-005 Allergen Management

## Scope
Covers raw materials, storage, dosing, production, changeovers, cleaning, rework, labelling and product release.

## Controls
1. Maintain an allergen register for all raw materials.
2. Assess cross-contact risks.
3. Apply spatial, time or organisational segregation.
4. Identify containers, tools and work-in-progress.
5. Control production sequencing.
6. Validate cleaning effectiveness where necessary.
7. Verify labels and packaging before start-up.
8. Control material and recipe changes.
9. Train relevant employees.
10. Investigate incidents and trends.

## Records
- allergen matrix,
- risk assessment,
- cleaning validation,
- changeover check,
- label verification,
- training records.
