## Sequence
1. Stop and safely isolate the equipment.
2. Remove product, packaging and waste.
3. Pre-clean.
4. Apply detergent according to supplier instructions.
5. Apply mechanical action to critical surfaces.
6. Rinse if required.
7. Apply disinfectant for the validated contact time.
8. Final rinse if required.
9. Dry / reassemble.
10. Complete pre-operational inspection.

## Verification
- visual inspection,
- rapid hygiene test where used,
- microbiological swabs according to plan,
- chemical-residue checks where relevant.

## Failure action
Repeat cleaning, prevent start-up, assess potentially affected product and record the deviation.
