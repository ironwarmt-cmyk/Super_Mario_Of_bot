# IN-001 Cleaning and Disinfection Instruction

## 1. Purpose
This instruction sets out how rooms, equipment, tools and surfaces are cleaned and disinfected at [SITE NAME] to remove soil, product and allergen residues and reduce microorganisms to a level that does not threaten food safety, without leaving chemical residues.

## 2. Scope
It applies to all production, packing, storage and welfare areas and to equipment and tools in contact with product. It covers manual cleaning, foam cleaning, CIP (cleaning in place) and allergen changeover cleaning. It applies to production staff, the hygiene team and external contractors.

## 3. Definitions
- **Cleaning**: removal of visible and invisible soil (product residues, fat, protein, scale) using water, detergent and mechanical action.
- **Disinfection**: reduction of microorganisms on a clean surface to a safe level using a product authorised as a biocide.
- **Cleaning factors (Sinner's circle)**: chemistry (concentration), temperature, time, mechanical action. Weakening one must be compensated by the others.
- **Contact surface**: a surface in direct contact with product or from which product may drip, fall or be transferred.
- **Cleaning verification**: confirmation of effectiveness (visual, ATP test, protein or allergen test, microbiological testing).

## 4. Responsibilities
- Production Manager: allows cleaning time in the production plan, supervises cleaning, releases the line for production.
- Hygiene team leader / operators: clean according to cleaning cards and record completion.
- Quality Manager: approves the cleaning schedule and chemicals, carries out verification (ATP, swabs), reviews trends, trains staff.
- Chemical store keeper: receives, stores and issues chemicals, maintains safety data sheets.
- Chemical supplier: advises on chemicals and concentrations, trains staff, provides technical and safety data sheets.

## 6. General rules
1. Cleaning follows the cleaning schedule (Appendix 1) and cleaning cards for each item and area (Appendix 2). Cleaning cards include: scope, frequency, chemicals and concentrations, temperature, time, tools, dismantling, PPE, cleanliness criteria and verification method.
2. Only chemicals approved by the Quality Manager, intended for the food industry and with a current safety data sheet are used. Disinfectants are authorised biocidal products in the country of use or the EU.
3. Cleaning and production do not take place at the same time in the same area. Exposed product and packaging are removed or protected before cleaning starts.
4. Low-pressure hoses are used where possible instead of high pressure to avoid spreading contaminated aerosols.
5. Cleaning tools are colour-coded by zone and purpose (Appendix 4), stored on racks, clean and dry, and replaced when worn.
6. The hygiene team cleans from top to bottom and from clean to dirty areas.

## 7. Cleaning and disinfection steps (standard clean)
1. **Preparation**: stop and isolate the equipment (lock-out/tag-out), remove product and packaging, protect electrical components.
2. **Dry clean**: remove solids dry (scrapers, brushes, industrial vacuum for dusts such as flour). Large residues are not washed into drains.
3. **Dismantling**: remove guards, knives, belts and parts as per the cleaning card; place parts on clean racks, not on the floor.
4. **Pre-rinse**: water at [e.g. 40–55°C] removes loose soil. Water that is too hot can bake protein onto surfaces.
5. **Detergent application**: apply detergent (foam, solution) at the concentration on the card, contact time [e.g. 10–20 min], mechanical action (brushes, pads). Detergent must not dry on the surface.
   - fat and protein soil: alkaline detergent,
   - scale and mineral deposits: acid detergent (per schedule, e.g. weekly).
6. **Rinse**: with potable water until the chemical is removed; check the surface is visually clean. If not, repeat steps 5–6.
7. **Disinfection**: apply disinfectant at the manufacturer's concentration and contact time.
8. **Final rinse** (if required by the disinfectant label): potable water.
9. **Drying and reassembly**: drain, dry (clean disposable towels, filtered compressed air), reassemble with clean hands or clean gloves.
10. **Check and record**: visual check by the operator, record on the card, check by the shift leader before release.

## 8. Chemical concentration control
1. Solutions are prepared from dosing units or using the dilution table on the card, never by eye.
2. Concentration is checked at least [e.g. weekly] by titration or test strips supplied by the chemical manufacturer; the result is recorded.
3. CIP systems monitor temperature, conductivity (concentration), time and flow; printouts or system data are archived.

## 9. Allergen changeover cleaning
1. After production with an allergen, before production without it, a full clean is carried out using the validated allergen cleaning card (PR-005).
2. Particular attention is paid to hard-to-reach areas: hoppers, screw conveyors, seals, nozzles, weighers, belt edges, packing machines.
3. Workwear and tools used during allergen production are changed or cleaned.
4. Effectiveness is verified with an allergen-specific or protein test (per PR-005) before line release. A positive result = re-clean and re-test.

## 10. Cleaning verification
| Method | Where | Frequency (example) | Criterion | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Visual inspection | all surfaces after cleaning | after every clean | no visible residues, deposits, standing water | shift leader |
| ATP test | selected contact points | [e.g. daily, 5 rotating points] | result below the threshold set for the luminometer and surface, e.g. ≤ [RLU] | quality |
| Microbiological swabs (total viable count, Enterobacteriaceae) | contact surfaces | [e.g. weekly, rotating] | per testing plan PR-017 | quality / laboratory |
| Environmental monitoring for Listeria spp. (RTE products) | zones 1–3 (contact and near-contact) | per testing plan PR-017 | not detected | quality / laboratory |
| Allergen test | lines after allergen changeover | after every changeover | negative | quality |
| Chemical residues (pH strips or chemical test) | after rinsing | [e.g. weekly] | rinse water pH as potable water | quality |

ATP thresholds are set by on-site validation (measurements on clean and soiled surfaces) and the luminometer manufacturer's guidance.

## 11. Cleaning programme validation
1. Before a new cleaning card is introduced and after a change of chemical, equipment or product, the method is confirmed effective: [e.g. 3] consecutive cleans with verification results within criteria.
2. The cleaning programme is reviewed at least annually using trends in ATP, swab results, complaints and GMP inspections.

## 12. Health and safety and chemical storage
1. Staff use the PPE stated on the safety data sheet (goggles or face shield, chemical-resistant gloves, apron).
2. Chemicals are stored in a locked, ventilated chemical store, away from food and packaging, in original labelled containers. Chemicals are never decanted into food containers.
3. Acids are never mixed with chlorine or alkalis (risk of toxic gas).
4. Spills are dealt with according to the safety data sheet.

## 13. Deviations
| Deviation | Immediate action | Corrective action | Decision by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surface visibly dirty after cleaning | re-clean, do not release line | review cleaning card, training | Shift leader |
| ATP result above threshold | re-clean, re-test | root cause (concentration, time, tools) | Quality Manager |
| Microbiological swab result out of criterion | enhanced cleaning and disinfection, re-swab, assess product impact | trend analysis, CAPA | Quality Manager |
| Listeria spp. detected on a contact surface | assess and hold product, intensive clean, vector swabbing | investigate source, CAPA, PR-013 if needed | Quality Manager |
| Positive allergen test | re-clean and re-test, line held | review allergen cleaning validation | Quality Manager |
| Wrong chemical concentration | correct dosing, assess cleans done with that solution | dosing unit check, training | Hygiene team leader |
| Chemical residue on surface | additional rinse, test, product assessment | training, rinse time control | Quality Manager |
