# PR-001 Document and Record Control

## Purpose
To ensure personnel use only current approved documents and that records are legible, protected, traceable and retrievable.

## Rules
1. Each controlled document has a code, title, version, date, owner and approver.
2. New documents and revisions are reviewed before release.
3. The controlled-document register is updated after every change.
4. Obsolete copies are removed from points of use or clearly marked as archived.
5. External documents are identified and periodically checked for currency.
6. Records are durable, legible, complete and attributable to date and person.
7. Record corrections preserve the original information.
8. Electronic access is role-based.
