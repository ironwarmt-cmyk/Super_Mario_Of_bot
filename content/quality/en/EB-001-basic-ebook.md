# BASIC e-book: Food Quality and Safety Management Foundations

> Implementation guide for people starting a food quality and food-safety system. It does not replace site-specific assessment.

## System pyramid
```text
        OBJECTIVES AND MANAGEMENT REVIEW
    PROCEDURES · WORK INSTRUCTIONS · SPECS
   HACCP · PRPs/GHPs · PROCESS CONTROL
RECORDS · EVIDENCE · TRAINING · RESPONSIBILITY
```

## 1. Why a quality system exists
A quality system is not a binder. It is a decision model: what is controlled, why, who owns it, what evidence remains and what happens when control is lost.

It protects food safety, legality, product quality and authenticity.

## 2. Four pillars
| Pillar | Control question | Example evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Safety | Can the product harm the consumer? | hazard analysis, monitoring, testing |
| Legality | Is the product legal for the target market? | approved label, specification |
| Quality | Does the product meet customer requirements? | checks, complaint trends |
| Authenticity | Is the declaration true? | supplier control, traceability |

## 3. HACCP in plain language
Start with product, process, intended use and consumer. Then map hazards and controls.

Minimum questions:
- What raw materials enter the product?
- Which allergens are present or could cross-contact?
- Which process step reduces risk?
- What happens if a control is missed?
- Which record proves control?

## HACCP decision flow
```text
Product → Process flow → Hazards → Risk → Controls → Monitoring → Verification
```

## 4. Practical documentation
Every procedure should answer: purpose, scope, responsibility, method, record and effectiveness check. Avoid vague wording unless frequency, owner, method and evidence are defined.

## 5. Records and evidence
Auditors assess evidence, not statements. Good records are legible, complete, dated, attributable and corrected without hiding the original entry.

## 6. First 30 days
| Week | Priority | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | process and document map | system map |
| 2 | hazard analysis and PRPs | food-safety plan draft |
| 3 | suppliers, cleaning, allergens, traceability | operating procedures |
| 4 | internal audit and CAPA | action plan |
