# PR-006 Internal Audits

## Purpose
To provide regular, independent verification of system effectiveness and operational practice.

## Programme
- Covers all relevant management-system areas.
- Frequency is based on risk, change and previous results.
- Audits are distributed through the year.
- Auditors should not audit their own work where this could affect impartiality.

## Process
1. Define scope and criteria.
2. Prepare checklist.
3. Interview, observe and review records.
4. Record objective evidence.
5. Classify findings using internal rules.
6. Assign actions and deadlines.
7. Verify closure and effectiveness.

## Records
- annual audit programme,
- audit plan,
- report,
- finding register,
- closure evidence.
