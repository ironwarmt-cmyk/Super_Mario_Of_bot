# PR-014 Food Defence and Site Security

## 1. Purpose
This procedure sets out how [SITE NAME] assesses and reduces the risk of deliberate contamination or damage to product, e.g. sabotage, vandalism, a disgruntled employee or an outsider, and how it protects grounds, buildings, materials and systems from unauthorised access.

## 2. Scope
It covers the site grounds, buildings, stores, goods-in and dispatch, production, sensitive materials (raw materials, packaging, chemicals, biocides), utilities (water, silos, tanks), IT and control systems, and employees, visitors, drivers and contractors.

## 3. Definitions
- **Food defence**: measures protecting food from deliberate contamination or damage motivated by ideology, revenge, extortion or other motives.
- **Threat assessment (TACCP)**: systematic assessment of who, where and how someone could deliberately harm product, and which measures prevent it.
- **Restricted area**: an area accessible only to authorised persons (e.g. silos, water intake, chemical store, server room).
- **Sealing**: securing a vehicle or tank with a numbered seal that is recorded.

## 4. Responsibilities
- Site Director: appoints the food defence team, approves the assessment and measures, provides resources.
- Food defence team (quality, maintenance, HR, warehouse, IT): carries out the threat assessment and reviews it at least annually.
- Quality Manager: coordinates, trains, tests procedures.
- HR: pre-employment checks as permitted by law, leaver procedure (return of cards and keys, account deactivation).
- Security / reception: access control, registration of visitors and vehicles.
- All employees: watch for unknown persons and unusual behaviour and report them.

## 5. References
- Regulation (EC) No 178/2002, Articles 14 and 17.
- BRCGS Food Safety Issue 9, clauses 4.2 (site security and food defence) and 4.1 (external standards and site security); IFS Food version 8, clause 4.21 (food defence).
- PAS 96:2017 "Guide to protecting and defending food and drink from deliberate attack" (BSI) – methodological guidance.
- Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) regarding CCTV and visitor data.
- PR-007, PR-013.

## 6. Threat assessment
1. The team walks through the process from receipt to dispatch and asks for each step:
   - Who has access (employees, drivers, contractors, visitors)?
   - Is product or material exposed and could something easily be added?
   - Would an addition be detected before dispatch?
   - What would the consequence be (health, number of consumers, reputation)?
2. Typical high-vulnerability points: bulk ingredient intake, silos and tanks, water intake, small-quantity additive mixing, chemical store, unattended loading docks, process control systems.
3. The assessment (Appendix 1) considers likelihood and impact; protective measures are defined for high-risk points.
4. The assessment is reviewed at least annually and after an incident, site layout change, new process or change in external threat level (e.g. threats against the sector).

## 7. Protective measures

### 7.1 Grounds and buildings
1. Fenced site, gates locked or controlled; site lighting at night.
2. Building entrances controlled (card, code, key). Emergency exits alarmed.
3. CCTV at key points (entrances, docks, silos) with signage per GDPR.
4. Keys and access cards logged; returned when an employee leaves.

### 7.2 Restricted areas
| Area | Protection | Who has access |
|---|---|---|
| Silos and tank hatches | locks, seals, opening log | [maintenance, designated operators] |
| Water intake and tanks | locked, monitored | [maintenance] |
| Chemical and biocide store | locked, issue logged | [chemical store keeper, hygiene team] |
| Small-quantity additive room | locked, usage log | [designated operators] |
| Server room, control systems | locked, personal passwords | [IT, maintenance] |

### 7.3 Personnel and visitors
1. Employees wear ID badges; visitors and contractors are registered, receive a visitor badge and are escorted in production.
2. New employees receive food defence training (PR-007). Agency workers follow the same rules.
3. Employees do not take personal items, bags or containers into production (lockers in changing room).
4. Open culture: concerns can be reported confidentially (QM-001, food safety culture).

### 7.4 Goods-in and dispatch
1. Deliveries are expected (booked). Unexpected deliveries are held until clarified.
2. Seals and pack integrity are checked; seal numbers are recorded.
3. Drivers wait in a designated area and do not enter stores unsupervised.
4. Finished goods vehicles are sealed and the seal number recorded on the dispatch note.

### 7.5 IT systems
1. Personal accounts, strong passwords, role-based permissions, deactivation when an employee leaves.
2. Backups, security updates, malware protection.
3. Remote access to control systems only via secure connection and for authorised persons.

## 8. Response
1. Suspected tampering (broken seal, signs of opening, unknown substance, threat letter) is reported immediately to the Quality Manager and Site Director.
2. Suspect product and material are held (PR-010) and secured as evidence. Evidence is not destroyed.
3. PR-013 is initiated, including notifying police if a crime is suspected.

## 9. Testing
At least annually the team tests selected measures (e.g. an attempted entry without a badge with Site Director approval, checking seals and logs, checking deactivation of former employees' accounts). Results are recorded and weaknesses handled through CAPA.

## 10. Deviations
| Deviation | Immediate action | Corrective action | Decision by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broken delivery seal | stop receipt, contact supplier, assess product | haulier review, reminder of rules | Quality Manager |
| Person without badge in production | stop, clarify, escort | review access control | Supervisor, security |
| Unlocked silo hatch | lock, assess risk to contents | review locks and opening log | Maintenance Manager, Quality Manager |
| Former employee's IT account active | deactivate, review logs | leaver checklist | IT Administrator |
| Threat or extortion | secure evidence, PR-013, police | review threat assessment, strengthen measures | Site Director |
