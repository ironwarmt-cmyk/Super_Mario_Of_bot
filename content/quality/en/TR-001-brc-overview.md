# Orientation Training: BRC Food - Management System Overview

> Original training material. It does not replace the official publication, interpretation guidance or licensed training.

## Learning objectives
Participants should understand:
- how certification systems for food sites work,
- management responsibility and food-safety culture,
- the role of HACCP and prerequisite programmes,
- documentation, records and internal audit,
- site, product, process and personnel controls,
- corrective action and continual improvement,
- how to build implementation evidence.

## Modules
1. Context and management responsibility
2. HACCP and risk management
3. Documents and records
4. Suppliers, specifications and traceability
5. Site conditions and hygiene
6. Allergens, authenticity and product control
7. Process control and calibration
8. Personnel competence
9. Internal audit, CAPA and audit readiness

## Exercise
Choose one site process and identify:
- 3 risks,
- 3 control evidences,
- 2 records,
- 1 effectiveness indicator.
