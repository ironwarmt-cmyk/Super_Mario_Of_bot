# PR-008 Pest Control, Waste Management, Storage and Transport

## 1. Purpose
This procedure sets out how [SITE NAME] prevents pests, manages waste, stores raw materials, packaging and products and controls transport, so that product is not contaminated, is kept in the required conditions and is issued in an order that maintains its shelf life.

## 2. Scope
It covers the site grounds and buildings, all storage areas (raw materials, packaging, chemicals, finished goods, including external stores), production waste, by-products, packaging and general waste, and own and contracted transport.

## 3. Definitions
- **Pests**: rodents, crawling and flying insects, birds and other animals that may contaminate product.
- **Integrated pest management (IPM)**: an approach prioritising prevention (building, hygiene, barriers) and monitoring, with chemicals used only when needed.
- **Bait station**: a tamper-resistant numbered container with bait or a trap.
- **FIFO / FEFO**: first in, first out / first expired, first out.
- **Animal by-products (ABP)**: materials of animal origin not intended for human consumption, subject to separate legislation.

## 4. Responsibilities
- Quality Manager: supervises the pest control contractor, reviews reports and trends, approves the device location plan.
- Pest control contractor (approved per PR-002): inspections, monitoring, treatments, reports, recommendations.
- Maintenance Manager: building repairs identified in reports (gaps, door seals, screens).
- Warehouse Manager: storage conditions, FIFO/FEFO, temperature monitoring, vehicle condition.
- All employees: report pest evidence immediately to their supervisor and the Quality Manager.

## 6. Pest control

### 6.1 Prevention
1. The building is sealed: external doors close and seal (gap under doors no more than [e.g. 6 mm]), opening windows and vents screened, service openings sealed.
2. Around the building, a vegetation-free strip or short grass ([e.g. 1 m]), no pallets or waste stored against walls.
3. Inside: no product residues in corners, spillages cleaned promptly, pallets and goods [e.g. 0.5 m] from walls to allow inspection.
4. Dock doors are opened only during loading; strip curtains or dock shelters are used.

### 6.2 Monitoring
1. The device location plan (Appendix 1) includes:
   - external bait or trap stations around the building,
   - mechanical traps or non-toxic monitoring stations inside (no toxic baits in production or open-product storage),
   - electric fly killers with glue boards (not zappers, to avoid fragmenting insects), located away from open product and external doors,
   - pheromone traps for stored product insects (e.g. moths, beetles) in dry goods stores.
2. Contractor visit frequency: [e.g. every 4–6 weeks] per contract and risk assessment; plus an annual in-depth survey by a biologist/specialist with trend review and recommendations (BRCGS requirement).
3. Fly killer glue boards are changed at least [e.g. 4 times a year] and tubes per manufacturer's guidance (e.g. annually before the season).

### 6.3 Treatments
1. Chemical treatments are carried out only by the contractor, using authorised products, with a safety data sheet and treatment details in the report.
2. Product, packaging and equipment are protected before treatment. Cleaning per IN-001 follows.

### 6.4 Reports and trends
1. After each visit the contractor provides a report: status of each device, activity, products used, recommendations.
2. The Quality Manager reviews the report within [e.g. 2 working days], enters recommendations in the action log and arranges repairs.
3. Activity trends by area are reviewed quarterly.

## 7. Waste management
1. Production waste bins are labelled, foot-operated with lids, in a colour distinct from product containers (e.g. red), emptied at least [e.g. once per shift] or when full.
2. Waste does not accumulate in production longer than necessary. Waste routes do not cross open product, or are separated in time.
3. External containers are closed, kept clean and on hard standing.
4. Waste is handed only to licensed operators, with records as required by national law.
5. Products with label errors, customer branding or destined for destruction are disposed of so they cannot re-enter the market; the disposal company confirms destruction.
6. By-products for animal feed or ABP are stored separately, labelled and handled per legislation.

## 8. Storage
1. Stores are clean, dry, lit and pest-proofed. Goods are stored on pallets or racks, not directly on the floor.
2. Separation: raw materials from finished goods, allergenic materials from others (per PR-005), chemicals in a separate store, held products in a designated, labelled area (PR-010).
3. Storage conditions (examples; specification and labelling values apply):

| Type | Conditions | Monitoring |
|---|---|---|
| Dry raw materials and packaging | ambient, relative humidity per specification | [e.g. daily] |
| Chiller | [e.g. 0–4°C] | continuous logger with alarm |
| Freezer | ≤ −18°C | continuous logger with alarm |
| Chemicals | per safety data sheet | visual at issue |

4. Temperature loggers are calibrated or checked per PR-009. Alarms reach [PERSON / PHONE] also outside working hours.
5. Issue follows FEFO. Short-dated or expired products are marked and held.
6. Open raw material packs are tightly closed and marked with opening date and use-by after opening.
7. External stores are controlled as service providers (PR-002), with a contract covering conditions, traceability and access to records.

## 9. Transport and dispatch
1. Before loading, the warehouse checks the vehicle: cleanliness, no odours or pests, body condition, refrigeration unit working and pre-cooled (for chilled transport), no incompatible loads (e.g. chemicals).
2. Chilled and frozen products are loaded quickly without breaking the cold chain; product and trailer temperatures are recorded on the loading sheet (Appendix 3).
3. The load is secured against damage; pallets wrapped and labelled.
4. External hauliers are approved service providers per PR-002; the contract specifies temperatures, vehicle cleaning, breakdown procedures and provision of temperature records.
5. Drivers at the dock follow visitor rules (PR-007) and do not enter production.

## 10. Deviations
| Deviation | Immediate action | Corrective action | Decision by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rodent evidence inside the building | call contractor within [e.g. 24 h], assess and hold product in range, clean and disinfect | find entry point, repair, increase monitoring | Quality Manager |
| Stored product insect activity in material | hold batch, check adjacent batches | complaint to supplier, treatment, stock rotation review | Quality Manager |
| Bird in the building | protect product, contractor removes bird | find entry point, repair | Quality Manager |
| Chiller temperature exceeded | assess duration and value, move stock, assess products | refrigeration service, alarm review | Quality Manager |
| Dirty or faulty vehicle | refuse loading | complaint to haulier, service provider review | Warehouse Manager |
| Overflowing waste bins in production | empty, clean | change collection frequency | Shift supervisor |
| Stock issued out of FEFO | assess remaining shelf life at customer | training, system control | Warehouse Manager |

## 11. Verification
GMP inspections (PR-006) cover pest evidence, waste and storage. The annual pest specialist survey and trend analysis are presented at management review.
