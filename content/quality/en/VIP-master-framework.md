# VIP - Complete Food Quality and Safety Management Framework

## How to use
Each section defines the business objective, minimum procedure scope, roles, inputs, actions, records, indicators and fields to tailor to the site.

## 1. Governance
Policy, objectives, product-safety culture, management review and change management.

## 2. Food safety plan
Multidisciplinary team, scope, prerequisites, product description, intended use, process flow, on-site flow verification, hazard analysis, controls, critical controls, limits, monitoring, corrective action, validation, verification and records.

## 3. Documents and records
Identification, approval, version control, distribution, archiving, electronic security and retention.

## 4. Internal audit
Risk-based annual programme, auditor competence, independence, reporting, CAPA and effectiveness verification.

## 5. Suppliers
Material risk assessment, qualification, approval, performance monitoring, changes, complaints and blocking.

## 6. Specifications
Controlled specifications for raw materials, packaging, process and finished products, with owner, approval, review and legal compliance.

## 7. Nonconformity and CAPA
Containment, impact assessment, product disposition, root cause, corrective action, owner, due date and effectiveness review.

## 8. Traceability and crisis
Lot identity, forward/backward tests, quantity reconciliation, crisis contacts, stock blocking, recovery, communication and post-event review.

## 9. Product protection
Intentional-threat assessment, access control, vulnerable areas, visitors, keys, alarms and breach response.

## 10. Authenticity
Raw-material vulnerability assessment, market intelligence, economic drivers, controls and review.

## 11. Layout and flows
Site map, people, materials, waste, rework, zoning, barriers and movement rules.

## 12. Infrastructure and utilities
Buildings, floors, walls, ceilings, drains, lighting, ventilation, water, ice, air and process gases.

## 13. Equipment and maintenance
Hygienic requirements, commissioning, planned maintenance, breakdowns, tools, lubricants, post-maintenance release and records.

## 14. Physical and chemical contamination
Glass, brittle plastic, metal, wood, chemicals, tools, detection equipment and breakage response.

## 15. Cleaning and disinfection
Sanitation schedule, instructions, chemicals, concentration, contact time, tools, validation, verification and failure response.

## 16. Pest, waste and storage
Pest programme, waste control, FIFO/FEFO, temperature, segregation and storage conditions.

## 17. Transport
Vehicle condition, cleanliness, temperature, load protection, inspections and records.

## 18. Product development
Brief, legal inputs, risk assessment, trials, process validation, shelf life, labelling and approval.

## 19. Labelling and allergens
Allergen matrix, cross-contact risk, segregation, production sequencing, cleaning validation, artwork approval and line checks.

## 20. Testing and release
Risk-based test plan, sampling, methods, laboratories, limits, trending, shelf life and product release.

## 21. Process control
Process specifications, parameters, limits, monitoring, alarms, breakdown response and product disposition.

## 22. Calibration
Equipment register, criticality, intervals, standards, out-of-tolerance results and product-impact review.

## 23. Personnel
Competence matrix, training, hygiene, health rules, protective clothing, visitors and contractors.

## 24. Enhanced-control areas
Applicability assessment, barriers, personnel flow, environmental monitoring, hygiene, clothing, equipment and maintenance.

## 25. Supporting forms
Audit checklist, CAPA form, complaint form, supplier assessment, traceability exercise, crisis report, sanitation plan, calibration log, training log, label check, release record, test plan and management review.

## VIP limits
- 30 tailored documents per month
- 30 hours of assistant access per month
- after document allowance is exhausted: 35 tokens per tailored document

## Tailoring rule
Every company-specific document should account for product, destination market, process, materials, allergens, zoning, equipment, traceability, testing, responsibilities and applicable legal requirements.
