# PR-003 Nonconformity, Root Cause and Corrective Action

## Workflow
1. Record the nonconformity.
2. Assess impact on product, consumer, legal compliance and process.
3. Contain the issue immediately.
4. Identify potentially affected lots or processes.
5. Complete root-cause analysis.
6. Define corrective and preventive actions.
7. Assign owner and deadline.
8. Verify implementation.
9. Check effectiveness after an agreed period.
10. Review trends for recurrence.

## Suggested methods
- 5 Why
- Cause-and-effect diagram
- Process analysis
- Change comparison
- Data and trend review
