# PR-010 Control of Non-conforming Product

## 1. Purpose
This procedure ensures that any raw material, packaging, intermediate or finished product at [SITE NAME] that does not meet specification, or is suspected of not meeting it, is promptly identified, labelled, isolated and not used or dispatched until an authorised person makes a documented decision.

## 2. Scope
It covers non-conforming product found at any stage: receipt, storage, production, packing, testing, complaints, customer returns, and products held due to missing records or uncertain status.

## 3. Definitions
- **Non-conforming product**: product that does not meet specification, legal, customer or process requirements (e.g. CCP deviation, wrong label, damaged packaging, test result out of limit).
- **Hold (quarantine)**: status in which product may not be used, processed or dispatched.
- **Rework**: reprocessing a product so that it meets requirements (e.g. repacking, re-passing through the detector).
- **Alternative use**: directing product to another acceptable use (e.g. as an ingredient in another product, animal feed) where safe and legal.
- **Concession**: documented approval to use or dispatch product with a deviation that does not affect safety or legality, agreed with the customer where relevant.

## 4. Responsibilities
- Every employee: stops suspect product and informs the supervisor.
- Shift supervisor / warehouse operative: labels and isolates product, sets the hold in the system, opens a report.
- Quality Manager: assesses product, decides on disposition, releases holds; agrees concessions with the customer.
- Production Manager and technologist: assess rework options.
- Site Director: decisions above [VALUE] or where in doubt.

## 5. References
- Regulation (EC) No 178/2002, Article 14 (food safety requirements) and Article 19.
- Regulation (EC) No 1069/2009 (where product becomes an animal by-product).
- BRCGS Food Safety Issue 9, clauses 3.8 (control of non-conforming product) and 5.7 (product release); IFS Food version 8, clause 5.10.
- HA-001, PR-003, PR-004, PR-013.

## 6. Identification and isolation
1. Suspect product is immediately labelled with a red "ON HOLD" label (Appendix 1) showing: name, batch, quantity, reason, date, signature.
2. The batch status in the warehouse system is changed to held to prevent issue.
3. Product is moved to a designated, labelled area (cage, rack or hold area). Where moving is impossible (e.g. silo, tank), the equipment is labelled and valves or issue locked.
4. Chilled and frozen products remain in required conditions until decision.
5. The full scope is determined: all batches, packaging, intermediates and products in which the item may have been used (using PR-004).
6. Each hold is entered in the non-conforming product register (Appendix 2) and numbered.

## 7. Assessment
The Quality Manager assesses the product within [e.g. 2 working days], considering:
- whether the non-conformity concerns safety (pathogens, allergens, foreign bodies, chemicals), legality (label, composition, quantity) or quality only (appearance, shape),
- test results, process records, loggers, technologist's opinion,
- customer requirements and contract,
- whether product may have left the site (then PR-013).

## 8. Possible decisions
| Decision | When acceptable | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Release without change | assessment shows the product meets requirements (e.g. false alarm, record completed with logger evidence) | rationale and evidence in the report |
| Concession | quality-only deviation, no effect on safety or legality | written customer approval where it concerns their specification |
| Rework | rework can restore conformity (e.g. repack, re-detect, sort) | rework instruction, post-rework check, traceability record, allergens considered |
| Alternative use | product safe but not meeting the original customer's requirements | legal compliance for the new use, customer branding removed |
| Return to supplier | non-conforming raw material or packaging | complaint, return note |
| Disposal | product unsafe or cannot be made conforming | handover to licensed operator, destruction certificate; customer-branded product destroyed so it cannot return to market |

Product with a safety non-conformity may not be released under a concession.

## 9. Releasing a hold
1. Holds are released only by the Quality Manager or a person authorised in writing (list in Appendix 3).
2. Release: red label removed, system status changed, register entry with decision, date and signature.
3. After rework, product undergoes the full checks of standard product before release (PR-012).

## 10. Root cause analysis
Every safety or legality non-conformity and recurring quality non-conformities are passed to PR-003 (CAPA). Monthly the Quality Manager analyses the register: number of holds, causes, costs, time to decision.

## 11. Deviations
| Deviation | Immediate action | Corrective action | Decision by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Held product dispatched | identify customer, assess, PR-013 if needed | system holds, training, CAPA | Quality Manager |
| Held product without label | re-label, check quantity | review rules, hold area checks | Warehouse Manager |
| Decision beyond deadline | escalate to Site Director | review workload, deputies | Quality Manager |
| Quantity in hold area differs from register | stock count, explain | CAPA | Quality Manager |

## 12. Verification
The hold area is checked during GMP inspections (match with register, labelling, conditions). The internal audit samples [e.g. 3] closed holds for justification of decisions.
