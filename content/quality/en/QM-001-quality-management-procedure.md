# QM-001 Food Quality and Safety Management Procedure

> Editable template. Replace all bracketed fields with site-specific information.

## 1. Purpose
To establish a controlled management system ensuring that products made by [COMPANY] are safe, legal, authentic, compliant with specification and consistently produced.

## 2. Scope
Applies to management, quality, production, purchasing, warehouse, engineering, sanitation, logistics, laboratory and all other functions that can affect product.

## 3. Responsibilities
- Site Director: approves policy, objectives, resources and management reviews.
- Quality Manager: maintains the system, documentation, audits, data review and improvement actions.
- Department Managers: implement controls in their areas.
- Employees: follow approved instructions and report deviations.

## 4. Policy and objectives
The company maintains a documented policy covering product safety, legality, authenticity, quality, customer responsibility and continual improvement. Objectives are measurable and assigned to an owner, deadline and indicator.

## 5. Risk management
Product and process risks are identified, assessed and controlled, including biological, chemical, physical, allergen, fraud, intentional and operational hazards.

## 6. System controls
The management system includes:
- approved procedures and instructions,
- current specifications,
- supplier controls,
- hazard analysis,
- internal audits,
- nonconformity and corrective action,
- traceability and recall readiness,
- complaints and trend analysis,
- hygiene and site conditions,
- product and process control,
- competence and training.

## 7. Management review
At least annually. Review inputs include audit results, objectives, complaints, incidents, nonconformities, corrective actions, testing, effectiveness of the food safety plan, resources and improvement priorities.

## 8. Communication
Relevant requirements are communicated to employees. Product safety or quality concerns can be reported through [REPORTING CHANNEL].

## 9. Records
- policy and objectives,
- management review minutes,
- action plan,
- KPI reports,
- decision and responsibility records.

## 10. Review
At least annually and after significant change to product, process, legislation, organisation or following a serious incident.
