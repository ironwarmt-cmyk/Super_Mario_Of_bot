# PRO — Product Design and Development

## Purpose
Ensure controlled development of new or changed products.

## Stages
1. Brief: customer, market, use, parameters, allergens, packaging, shelf life.
2. Legal and claim review.
3. Preliminary hazard and process-risk assessment.
4. Laboratory/pilot trials.
5. Recipe and specification verification.
6. Process and critical-parameter validation.
7. Shelf-life determination.
8. Label and packaging approval.
9. Industrial trial.
10. Formal launch approval.
11. Post-launch review.

## Records
Brief, trial records, risk assessment, approved recipe, specification, label, shelf-life report and launch approval.
