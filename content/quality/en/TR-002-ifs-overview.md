# Orientation Training: IFS Food - Management System Overview

> Original orientation material. It does not replace the official standard or training approved by the scheme owner.

## Learning objectives
Participants learn:
- process-based management and governance,
- food safety and quality management,
- HACCP based on Codex principles,
- resource and competence management,
- operational planning and control,
- measurement, data analysis and improvement,
- building objective evidence for assessment.

## Modules
1. Governance and responsibility
2. Quality and food safety management
3. HACCP and hygienic prerequisites
4. Resource management
5. Production-process realisation
6. Product and process control
7. Measurement, audit, analysis and improvement
8. Audit readiness and nonconformity management

## Exercise
Map one process: inputs -> risks -> control -> record -> responsible person -> performance indicator.
