# PR-002 Supplier Approval and Monitoring

## 1. Purpose
This procedure sets out how risk assessment, approval, monitoring and periodic review of suppliers of raw materials, packaging, services and outsourced processes are carried out, so that everything affecting [SITE NAME] products is safe, legal, authentic and to specification.

## 2. Scope
It covers suppliers and manufacturers of:
- raw materials, intermediates, additives, flavourings, processing aids,
- food contact packaging and secondary packaging,
- critical services: pest control, external cleaning, laundry, transport and storage, laboratories, equipment servicing, waste disposal,
- outsourced processes (e.g. packing, processing at a subcontractor),
- agents and brokers.

## 3. Definitions
- **Approved supplier**: a supplier that has passed assessment and is on the approved supplier list.
- **Conditional supplier**: a supplier temporarily accepted with additional controls until full approval.
- **Raw material risk assessment**: assessment of safety, allergen, authenticity and quality hazards associated with a raw material and its origin.
- **Specification**: agreed document setting out requirements for a material or service.
- **Agent / broker**: a party that sells a product but does not manufacture or pack it.

## 4. Responsibilities
- Purchasing Manager: sources suppliers, collects documents, maintains the approved supplier list, communicates requirements to suppliers.
- Quality Manager: carries out raw material and supplier risk assessment, decides on approval, plans supplier audits, conducts periodic review.
- Technologist: agrees technical specifications, assesses the effect of supplier changes on product.
- Warehouse: accepts deliveries only from approved suppliers and carries out goods-in checks.
- Site Director: approves exceptions (e.g. emergency supplier without full assessment).

## 5. References
- Regulation (EC) No 178/2002, Articles 17–19.
- Regulation (EC) No 852/2004, Annex II, Chapter IX.
- Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004 (food contact materials), Commission Regulation (EC) No 2023/2006 (GMP for materials), Commission Regulation (EU) No 10/2011 (plastics).
- BRCGS Food Safety Issue 9, section 3.5; IFS Food version 8, clauses 4.4 and 4.5.
- HA-001, PR-005, PR-015.

## 6. Raw material and service risk assessment
1. For each raw material and packaging item, the Quality Manager completes a risk assessment (Appendix 1) considering:
   - biological, chemical and physical hazards and allergens,
   - risk of adulteration and substitution (PR-015),
   - origin, length and complexity of the supply chain,
   - supplier history (complaints, test results, RASFF alerts),
   - impact on finished product safety (e.g. whether it is heat-treated later).
2. The result determines the supplier category and assessment method:

| Risk category | Examples | Approval method | Re-assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | ready-to-eat materials without further processing, allergenic materials, spices, nuts, primary packaging | valid GFSI-benchmarked certificate (e.g. BRCGS, IFS, FSSC 22000) or supplier audit, questionnaire, specification, testing of first deliveries | annually |
| Medium | materials that are heat-treated, secondary packaging, chilled transport | GFSI certificate or questionnaire with evidence, specification | every 2 years |
| Low | low-risk dry materials, services without product contact | questionnaire, specification | every 3 years |

3. The risk assessment is updated at least annually and after any incident, change of origin, legislative change or new hazard information.

## 7. New supplier approval
1. The Purchasing Manager communicates requirements and collects:
   - supplier questionnaire (Appendix 2),
   - copy of a valid certificate with scope (verify in the certification body database, e.g. BRCGS Directory, IFS Database),
   - registration or approval number (approval number for products of animal origin),
   - product specification, allergen declaration, safety data sheet (for chemicals),
   - for packaging: declaration of compliance with Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004 and, where applicable, (EU) No 10/2011, with migration test results,
   - for services: licences, insurance, scope of service, staff competence.
2. The Quality Manager assesses completeness, audit or certificate results and any non-conformities.
3. For high-risk suppliers without GFSI certification, the site audits the supplier using a competent auditor (internal or external).
4. The decision (approved / conditional / rejected) is recorded on the assessment form (Appendix 3). Approved suppliers are added to the list (Appendix 4) with scope: specific products and manufacturing sites.
5. Conditional supplier: maximum [e.g. 3 months] or [e.g. 3 deliveries], each with additional checks or testing.

## 8. Agents and brokers
The site knows the manufacturing site of every raw material. The agent provides manufacturer and site details and ensures the manufacturer meets this procedure. Without this data the material is not released for use.

## 9. Customer-nominated suppliers
Where the customer requires a specific supplier, the site still collects basic documents (specification, allergen declaration, certificate) and monitors delivery quality. Responsibility for assessment is agreed with the customer in writing.

## 10. Specifications
1. Each raw material and packaging item has an agreed specification with: description, composition, allergens, physico-chemical and microbiological parameters with limits, storage conditions, shelf life, packaging and labelling, acceptance criteria and legal requirements.
2. Specifications are reviewed every [e.g. 3 years] or on any change. The supplier must notify changes in composition, process, manufacturing site or origin before implementation.

## 11. Delivery monitoring
1. At receipt, the warehouse checks: compliance with the order and approved supplier list, vehicle condition (cleanliness, odour, pests, temperature), packaging and seals, labels, shelf life, batch number, documents (certificate of analysis where required).
2. Receipt temperatures (examples, confirm against specification): chilled products as declared by the manufacturer, e.g. ≤ 4°C; frozen products ≤ −18°C with tolerances set by transport legislation.
3. Non-conforming deliveries are held and labelled according to PR-010, and a complaint is raised with the supplier.
4. The raw material testing plan (PR-017) reflects risk category; results are compared with the specification and supplier certificates of analysis.

## 12. Periodic supplier review
1. The Quality Manager reviews suppliers at least annually using KPIs (Appendix 5).
2. Outcome:
   - ≥ [e.g. 85 points]: approved,
   - [e.g. 70–84 points]: conditional, supplier action plan required,
   - < [e.g. 70 points] or a serious safety incident: suspension and decision on delisting.
3. Certificate validity is monitored continuously; warehouse and purchasing are informed of supplier certificate suspension within [e.g. 1 working day].

## 13. Deviations
| Deviation | Immediate action | Corrective action | Decision by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery from a supplier not on the list | hold, do not book into stock | supplier assessment or return, root cause in purchasing | Quality Manager |
| Expired or suspended certificate | stop orders, assess stock risk | request explanation, audit, possible conditional acceptance | Quality Manager |
| Raw material test result out of specification | hold batch, assess products where it was used | complaint, increased checks, supplier review | Quality Manager |
| Unannounced change at supplier | stop use of changed material | change assessment (allergens, label, HACCP) | Quality Manager, Technologist |
| Emergency supplier without full assessment | limited quantity, additional checks, Site Director approval | complete documents by deadline | Site Director |

## 14. Verification
1. Annually the Quality Manager checks that every raw material in recipes has an approved supplier, a current specification and a risk assessment.
2. The internal audit (PR-006) covers purchasing and goods-in.
3. Supplier performance results are presented at management review.
