# PR-002 Supplier Approval and Monitoring

## 1. Risk classification
Suppliers of raw materials, packaging and services are classified according to their potential impact on product safety, legality, authenticity and quality.

## 2. Approval evidence
Depending on risk, approval may use:
- recognised certification,
- supplier audit,
- questionnaire and document review,
- test results and specifications,
- performance and complaint history.

## 3. Criteria
Review can include:
- specification compliance,
- delivery and test performance,
- complaints,
- documentation completeness,
- traceability,
- supply-chain security,
- process or site changes.

## 4. Monitoring
Performance is reviewed at a frequency based on risk. Poor-performing suppliers receive action plans, conditional approval or blocking.

## 5. Records
- Approved supplier list
- Risk assessment
- Questionnaires / audit reports
- Performance review
- Corrective actions
