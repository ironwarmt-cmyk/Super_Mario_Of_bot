# PRO — Labelling, Packaging and Product Release

## Purpose
Ensure correct packaging/labels are used and only compliant product is released.

## Controls
1. Maintain approved label masters.
2. Formally approve every change.
3. At start-up/changeover verify product code, label, date, lot and legal information.
4. Control disposal of obsolete printed materials.
5. Define line-check frequency.
6. Before release review required quality, process and laboratory results.
7. Release only by authorised personnel.
8. Deviations trigger hold and scope assessment.

## Records
Label master, start-up check, line checks, change log and product release record.
