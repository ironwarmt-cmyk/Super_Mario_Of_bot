# PR-004 Traceability, Withdrawal and Recall

## Purpose
To enable rapid identification of raw materials, packaging, production lots and customers receiving finished product.

## Traceability system
Each lot identifier should link at least to:
- supplier and raw-material lot,
- direct packaging and legal label,
- date / shift / line,
- processing records,
- checks and test results,
- quantities made, rejected and dispatched,
- customer.

## Exercise
At least annually, test traceability in both directions and perform quantity reconciliation. Establish an internal completion target, for example within four hours.

## Withdrawal / recall
1. Activate the crisis team.
2. Block affected stock.
3. Identify lots and customers.
4. Assess risk and legal duties.
5. Define communications.
6. Track recovered quantities.
7. Close the event with root-cause and effectiveness review.

## Records
- traceability exercise report,
- quantity reconciliation,
- crisis contact list,
- incident report,
- communications and decisions.
