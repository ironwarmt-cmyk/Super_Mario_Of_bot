# PR-004 Traceability, Product Withdrawal and Recall

## 1. Purpose
This procedure ensures that [SITE NAME] can quickly establish which raw materials and packaging each finished batch was made from, how it was produced and where it went, and can withdraw or recall product efficiently if it threatens safety or does not meet legal or customer requirements.

## 2. Scope
It covers the whole chain: from the supplier (one step back), through receipt, storage, production, rework, packing and finished goods storage, to the customer (one step forward). It applies to raw materials, food contact packaging, intermediates, rework, finished products and returned products.

## 3. Definitions
- **Traceability**: the ability to trace and follow a food and its ingredients through all stages of production, processing and distribution (Article 3(15) of Regulation (EC) No 178/2002).
- **Batch (lot)**: a quantity of product produced under practically identical conditions and identified with the same number.
- **Mass balance**: comparison of raw materials received and used with finished product, waste and stock.
- **Withdrawal**: removal of product from the distribution chain before it reaches the consumer.
- **Recall**: action in which product is also retrieved from consumers, who are informed.

## 4. Responsibilities
- Quality Manager: owner of the traceability system, organises tests, leads the crisis team in a withdrawal (with the Site Director).
- Warehouse: records batches at receipt and dispatch, applies FIFO / FEFO.
- Production: records batches of raw materials, packaging and rework used on the production report and assigns finished product batch numbers.
- Sales / logistics: keeps dispatch records with batch numbers and customer details; contacts customers during withdrawals.
- Site Director: decides on withdrawal or recall, contacts authorities and media (PR-013).

## 6. Batch coding
1. Raw materials and packaging: the supplier batch number is retained and an internal receipt number [FORMAT, e.g. R-YYMMDD-NN] is assigned and written on the warehouse label and in the system.
2. Finished product: batch number assigned using [FORMAT, e.g. YYDDD-L-S: year, day of year, line, shift]. A batch may not cover more than [e.g. one shift on one line].
3. Intermediates and rework: labelled with name, batch number, production date, use-by date and allergens.
4. The finished product batch number is printed on primary and secondary packaging legibly and permanently.

## 8. Traceability test
1. A test is carried out at least annually (recommended every six months, alternating forward and backward), on a randomly selected product and raw material, including an allergenic material or one involving rework.
2. The test covers:
   - backward: from finished batch to raw material and packaging batches and suppliers,
   - forward: from raw material batch to all finished batches and customers,
   - mass balance for the selected material,
   - full process records (CCPs, cleaning, release).
3. Target time for complete data: no more than [e.g. 4 hours]. Customer requirements may be stricter.
4. Mass balance should close within [e.g. ± 2%] allowing for process losses. A greater difference requires explanation and CAPA.
5. Results are recorded in the report (Appendix 1). Any problem (missing record, excessive time) is a non-conformity under PR-003.

## 9. Withdrawal and recall

### 9.1 Triggers
A decision is considered when there is evidence or reasonable suspicion that product:
- is injurious to health (e.g. pathogen, foreign body, undeclared allergen, contaminant above limit),
- does not meet legal requirements (e.g. wrong label, unauthorised substance),
- does not meet essential customer quality requirements.

### 9.3 Process
1. Problem reported to the Quality Manager (any source: complaint, test result, supplier notice, RASFF, authority).
2. Crisis team convened within [e.g. 1 hour].
3. Facts gathered: product, batches, quantities produced, in stock and dispatched, customers (using section 7).
4. Risk assessment and decision: no action / internal hold / withdrawal / consumer recall.
5. Immediate hold of all stock on site and in external warehouses.
6. Customers notified (phone, then e-mail with form, Appendix 2) and asked to stop sale, count stock and confirm.
7. Authorities notified: where product may be injurious to health or does not meet safety requirements, the operator immediately informs the competent authority, in accordance with Article 19 of Regulation (EC) No 178/2002.
8. For a recall: consumer notice (content agreed with the authority), published through agreed channels.
9. Certification body notified within the timeframe required by the standard (e.g. BRCGS: within 3 working days of the decision to recall; IFS: per IFS and certification body requirements).
10. Collection, storage and reconciliation of product (produced = in stock + recovered + sold to consumers + destroyed).
11. Decision on product disposition (disposal, rework, alternative use) with a record.
12. Root cause analysis and CAPA (PR-003), final report (Appendix 3).

## 10. Withdrawal test
1. At least annually a withdrawal is simulated with the crisis team, covering: contact list currency, reconstruction of distribution of a selected batch, simulated customer contact (without triggering unnecessary action at the customer, or with prior warning).
2. The time for each stage is measured. Lessons are recorded and implemented through CAPA.

## 11. Deviations
| Deviation | Immediate action | Corrective action | Decision by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Missing raw material batch number on production report | establish from warehouse issues, assess scope of finished batches | training, form change, barcode scanning | Quality Manager |
| Mass balance outside limit | explain the difference (losses, weighing errors, unrecorded rework) | correct recording process, CAPA | Quality Manager |
| Test took longer than target | review the process, identify bottleneck | system or form changes | Quality Manager |
| Illegible batch code on pack | stop packing, sort | printer review, code check at start and every [x] min | Production Manager |
| Out-of-date contact list | update immediately | calendar reminder for reviews | Quality Manager |

## 12. Verification
Results of traceability tests and withdrawal simulations are presented at management review. The internal audit covers this process at least annually.
