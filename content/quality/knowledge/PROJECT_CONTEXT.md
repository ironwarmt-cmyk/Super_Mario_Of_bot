# Quality Assurance Support — project context for AI

## Product purpose
Quality Assurance Support is a bilingual PL/EN Telegram bot and embedded Quality Hub for food-industry quality and food-safety professionals.

## Quality-document expectation
Every generated document must look and behave like a controlled quality-system document, not a block of prose. Use:
- document code and title,
- version and issue/effective date,
- owner / prepared by / approved by,
- purpose and scope,
- clear responsibilities,
- risk-based method and operational steps,
- acceptance criteria and escalation,
- records and retention,
- verification / validation where applicable,
- effectiveness/KPI review,
- revision history,
- editable site-specific placeholders.

Avoid vague phrases such as "regularly", "as required" and "where appropriate" unless the document also defines who decides, the trigger/frequency, the method, acceptance criterion and retained evidence.

## Core product areas
The library and assistant support:
- quality and food-safety management,
- HACCP / GHP / PRP,
- document and record control,
- supplier and raw-material approval,
- nonconformities, RCA and CAPA,
- traceability, withdrawal and recall,
- allergen management,
- internal audits and gap analysis,
- complaints and trend analysis,
- authenticity / food fraud vulnerability,
- product protection / food defence,
- incident and crisis management,
- product development and change control,
- labelling, packaging and release,
- testing and laboratory controls,
- maintenance and calibration,
- pest management, waste, storage and transport,
- personnel competence, hygiene and protective clothing,
- management review, KPI and continual improvement.

## Source discipline
For current external requirements, the assistant must verify official current sources before answering. The local library is an implementation aid, not evidence that an external requirement is current.

## Language
Respond in the user's selected language. Polish copy should sound like a professional Polish food-industry Quality Manager. English copy should use natural international food-safety/quality terminology rather than literal translations.
